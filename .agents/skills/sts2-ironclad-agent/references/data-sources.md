## Authority order

1. The current Mod snapshot and current installed `release_info.json` for this run's actions, card text, costs, enemy intents, event choices, shop inventory, map edges and offered rewards.
2. Mega Crit's Steam patch notes and official Neowsletters for version changes.
3. Version-selected game-data indexes that describe themselves as extracted from game files. Use them as a research aid and cross-check the branch selector.
4. Player guides and tier lists for strategic hypotheses only; note branch and publication/update date, and do not present opinions as rules.

## Current combat synthesis for this project

The supported guides do not justify “always attack” or “always block,” and none gives a reliable numeric HP threshold for low-Ascension Ironclad. They do support front-loaded damage as a run-level early-fight plan, keeping enough Block to survive dangerous turns, treating kills as tempo/defense, and paying HP for a return such as ending a fight sooner. This project does not turn that plan into an attack preference for an individual turn: a `usable` same-threat reserve is not an attack bonus. Compare actual enemy HP damage, HP loss prevented by Block, kill-canceled attacks, remaining enemy HP, and Energy opportunity cost; material defense can be preferable to small nonlethal damage, while damage is useful when threat is light and it materially advances or ends the fight. Attack labels and zero-HP-damage plays get no bonus. Each exact line compares post-turn HP with its remaining unblocked visible attack damage; a nonpositive difference is marked `critical` as a same-threat stress proxy, not a forecast of the next turn or a threshold asserted by any guide. The evaluator reports exact complete-line outcomes separately and never converts enemy damage and player HP loss at a fixed rate. Known lethal lines are removed when an exact surviving line exists. Real attack/defense trades go to KEV with current HP, intents, enemy HP/Block, and kill effects visible. Pure persistent Strength without same-turn HP-damage payoff is filtered instead of being played on the basis of visible future attacks: future draw, Energy, remaining fight length, and whether an attack clears Block cannot be established from pile contents. Main `SetupStrike` applies its temporary Strength after its own hit, so only later same-turn attacks can use it. Unmodeled cards and potions remain separate unresolved choices; their existence cannot invalidate an exact line result. Effects outside the bounded evaluator remain uncertain, not zero.

The [Luna-only Ironclad Skill](../../sts2-ironclad-luna/SKILL.md) uses deck-aware card-pick priors and strategy sources only for Luna's real strategic decisions. Community tier lists remain contextual priors; they never replace the complete live deck or current installed data.
